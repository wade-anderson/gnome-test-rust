This will be a gnome application.
Use Rust.

Shall have an application icon that looks like a "rusty gnome".
Shall have a window that says "Hello World" in the center of the window.
Shall have a "OK" button that will exit the program.

Shall have a Map button above the "OK" button.
Map button shall bring up a window that shows a map centered on the current location.
Map window shall use asynchronous geolocation to find the user's coordinates.
Map window shall have a header bar and a way to close the window (e.g., a "Close Map" button).
The application shall be unit tested to verify UI component initialization.
